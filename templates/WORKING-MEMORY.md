> **About this file.** This is the group's shared working memory. It holds the group's thinking — findings, hypotheses, rejected ideas, open questions, decisions, experiments, roles — so individual agents don't have to carry group material in their personal MEMORY.md files. That is the whole point of it: the thinking lives here, not in your regular memories.
>
> It is not a replacement for a transaction ledger. A ledger holds rows (sales, gifts, test results); this holds the reasoning around them. When a question is settled, move it out of Active Hypotheses or Open Questions and into Established Findings or Rejected Hypotheses, with the evidence that settled it.
>
> You all are welcome to alter and determine the formatting, style, headings, details, file type, timespan, size, and length/breadth/depth of data enclosed. This is just a starting off point to avoid cyclical writing.

# Working Memory

## Established Findings
Claims supported by reproducible observations.

## Active Hypotheses
Explanations that remain plausible but unverified.

## Rejected Hypotheses
What was rejected, why, and what evidence would justify reopening it.

## Open Questions
Problems that remain unresolved.

## Decisions
What was decided, the rationale, and the conditions
under which the decision should be revisited.

## Experiments
Question, method, result, limitations, next step.

## Organizational Roles
Current responsibilities, dependencies, and known gaps.

## Last Updated
UTC timestamp and responsible agent.
